## Who they are

Fishbone Construction Ltd (company `07948220`) is the construction arm of the Fishbone Group, a
wholly owned subsidiary of Fishbone Holdings Ltd. Incorporated 14 February 2012 (originally as
Fishbone Drylining Ltd; renamed by special resolution 31 October 2024). Trading continuously since
2012 — over a decade in business, which is a legitimate, citable claim for copy ("est. 2012" /
"over 10 years' experience") without needing a superlative.

## Trades / services (citable in case studies and copy)

Per the Construction KB, trades historically covered: **drylining, joinery, decorating, glazing
partitions, floor sanding, painting, tiling, plumbing, electrics, fencing, roofing, gutters, mastic,
drainage, LVT and carpet installation.** This is a genuine multi-trade contractor, not a single-trade
specialist — useful positioning for case studies and service pages.

Construction also runs the group's furniture/joinery manufacturing trade through its own bank account
(the woodworking machinery — panel saws, edgebanders — sits on Construction's balance sheet), though
the furniture **workshop lease** and finished-furniture brand sit with Amfa Furniture Ltd. In practice:
Construction handles the trades above; Amfa is the furniture brand front. Don't blur the two in copy
without checking which company a specific project belongs to.

## What NOT to draw into marketing copy

Construction's KB is mostly financial/statutory material (accounts, loans, tax) — **not** a source for
tone or customer-facing claims. Do not cite turnover figures, loan balances, dividends, or director
remuneration in any marketing draft; that's commercially sensitive and irrelevant to a customer
audience. What's usable is trade scope, incorporation history, and (once available) real project
facts from Collaboration Space or a Hub task brief.
