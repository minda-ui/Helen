## Voice & tone (real patterns to imitate)

Straightforward, confident, no-nonsense — short declarative sentences, plain language, always ties a
claim back to a concrete practice (a step, a check, a habit) rather than a bare superlative. UK spelling.
Recurring emphasis words: *reliable, clear, steady, straightforward, accountable, no shortcuts*.

Real example lines worth imitating directly:
- *"Quick replies, clear answers, and regular updates — so you always know what's done and what's coming
  next."*
- *"No shortcuts, no 'good enough,' just reliable workmanship you can trust."*
- *"We set realistic dates, keep steady progress, and flag changes early — no last-minute surprises."*
- *"Every customer is individual. Every idea is unique."*

Three recurring differentiators (use consistently, don't invent others): **Straightforward
communication** (regular updates, early heads-up on decisions), **Experienced craftsmanship** (clean
lines, solid structure, proper preparation, no shortcuts), **Timelines you can plan around** (realistic
scheduling, steady pace, changes flagged early).

## Process (site's own two framings — use whichever fits the content length)

**Three-phase (About Us):** Project Assessment (site visit, measurements, technical consultation,
condition evaluation, timeline planning) → Construction (site setup, scheduling, coordination,
installation, quality control) → Handover (final inspections, punch-list, cleaning, walkthrough,
warranty support).

**Six-step (Home, "From Brief to Handover"):**
1. Request & brief — client shares goal, deadline, constraints
2. Site visit — space assessed, measurements taken, requirements confirmed
3. Quote & plan — clear scope, cost breakdown, schedule
4. Execution — built in stages, site kept tidy, plan followed
5. Updates — regular progress notes/photos, early heads-up if a decision is needed
6. Final check & handover — joint review, punch-list, clean handover
