## Post 2 — How we work (process-led)

A clear process, managed at every step.

From that first consultation to final installation, we handle everything in-house — no handing your project between third parties.

1️⃣ Consultation — we talk through your space, your style, your budget
2️⃣ Design & material selection — a full 3D visualisation before anything is made
3️⃣ Manufacturing — built on our own equipment, precisely to your design

## Post 3 — Bespoke or fitted: there's an AMFA option for every space

Not every kitchen project needs the same approach — and we build both.

🔹 **Bespoke kitchens** — fully custom, no fixed sizes or templates. Perfect for non-standard layouts, premium interiors, large family kitchens or a genuinely unique design.

🔹 **Fitted kitchens** — built-in, made to standard sizes and modules, which keeps things quicker and more affordable — still with your choice of finish, colour and layout. A great fit for apartments, new-build homes and budget-conscious projects.

Whichever route suits your space, it's designed around you, manufactured in our own Newcastle workshop, and installed by our own team.

Get a free quote, or book a visit to the showroom — whichever works best for you.
