## A Tough Fit-Out. A Smooth Result.

### Fishbone Construction's Rolex retail fit-out

When Rolex needed a retail unit converted and ready to open, the brief was blunt: limited time, strict requirements, and zero room for mistakes. That's the kind of job Fishbone Construction takes on — no shortcuts, no "good enough," just reliable workmanship you can trust.

**The work.** A full interior fit-out: drylining, painting, joinery, taping and demolition, all coordinated on one site under one clear plan.

**Clean Site, Clear Process.** Every stage was carefully planned before it started, with organised site management and protection for finished surfaces as the job progressed. The site was cleaned daily, and the team chose durable materials and solid fixing methods built for the wear a busy retail unit takes over time — not just for how it looks on handover day.

**Built to Last.** The result speaks for itself: neat lines, clean details, and a space that feels ready for customers from day one.

This is one entry in a wider portfolio of retail and commercial fit-out work, alongside completed projects for Armani, Versace, Superdry, Peloton, Sofa.com and others.
