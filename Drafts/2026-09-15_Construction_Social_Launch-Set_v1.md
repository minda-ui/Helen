## Post 2 — How we work (process-led)

From brief to handover, here's what to expect:

1️⃣ Request & brief — you share your goal, deadline and constraints
2️⃣ Site visit — we assess the space, take measurements, confirm requirements
3️⃣ Quote & plan — clear scope, cost breakdown, schedule
4️⃣ Execution — built in stages, site kept tidy, plan followed
5️⃣ Updates — regular progress notes and photos, early heads-up if a decision's needed
6️⃣ Final check & handover — joint review, punch-list, clean handover

We set realistic dates, keep steady progress, and flag changes early — no last-minute surprises.

Quick replies, clear answers, and regular updates, so you always know what's done and what's coming next.
