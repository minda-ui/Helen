## 1. Living & Media Furniture

### Manufacturing time, pricing and warranty

The average manufacturing time for furniture is **4–8 weeks** after the design and materials have
been approved. Production time depends on:
- The complexity of the project
- The materials selected
- Non-standard sizes
- Custom finishes (media wall integrations, LED lighting, electric fireplace surrounds)
- The availability of special order components

The cost is calculated individually and depends on factors such as:
**Prices from £1,500**
- The size and scope of the piece (a single TV unit vs. a full media wall composition)
- The materials selected
- The finish options
- The number of interior elements
- The complexity of the design

We provide a warranty of up to 10 years on the furniture we manufacture and the hardware used
(depending on the materials and manufacturers selected). The warranty covers manufacturing defects and
workmanship quality, provided the furniture is used and maintained correctly.

### FAQ

**Can you build a media wall around an existing TV and sound system?**
Yes. We plan the layout around your equipment, sockets, cable management, and ventilation requirements
as part of the design stage.

**Do you provide installation?**
Yes. We offer a complete service, from design and manufacturing through to professional installation.

**Can I order a single TV unit rather than a full media wall?**
Yes. If a full media wall isn't required, we can manufacture a custom TV unit in any configuration —
wall-mounted, freestanding, or a long modern console.

**How long does manufacturing take?**
On average, production takes **4–8 weeks** after final design approval.

**Do you provide a warranty?**
Yes. All furniture comes with a warranty covering workmanship and materials, in accordance with the
company's warranty terms.

The average manufacturing time is **4–8 weeks** after the design and materials have been approved.
Production time depends on:
- The complexity and scale of the project
- The materials selected
- Non-standard sizes or an unusual space (alcoves, sloped ceilings, awkward corners)
- The availability of special order components

The cost is calculated individually and depends on factors such as:
**Prices from £1,500**
- The size and scope of the storage solution
- The materials selected

- The interior fittings and finish options

We provide a warranty of up to 10 years on the furniture we manufacture and the hardware used
(depending on the materials and manufacturers selected). The warranty covers manufacturing defects and

### FAQ

**What kind of spaces can you design storage for?**
Any space that doesn't fit neatly into a standard kitchen, bedroom, or living room category —
alcoves, awkward corners, understairs areas, sloped ceilings, and other non-standard architectural
features.

**Is every project fully bespoke?**
Yes. Every custom storage project is developed from scratch, based on the room's dimensions,
architectural features, intended use, and your storage requirements.

**Do you provide installation?**
Yes. We offer a complete service, from design and manufacturing through to professional installation.

**How long does manufacturing take?**
On average, production takes **4–8 weeks** after final design approval.

**Do you provide a warranty?**
Yes. All furniture comes with a warranty covering workmanship and materials, in accordance with the
company's warranty terms.
