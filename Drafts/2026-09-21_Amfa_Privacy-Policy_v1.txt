4. HOW WE USE YOUR DATA

We use your personal data to:
- respond to your enquiry or quote request;
- arrange and carry out measuring visits and consultations;
- provide you with a quote and, if you go ahead, deliver the furniture and installation service you
  order;
- keep you updated on the status of your order;
- improve our website and services.

Our legal basis for this is that it is necessary to take steps at your request before entering into a
contract with you, and/or to perform a contract with you once you place an order. Where we use your
data for anything else (for example, to tell you about other Amfa products or services), we will only
do so with your consent, and you can withdraw that consent at any time.
