## 1. Geography and audience

Amfa's free measuring visit covers a 50-mile radius of the Newcastle showroom (`Brand-and-Voice/Amfa-Furniture-Ltd.md`) — recommend targeting Newcastle upon Tyne and the surrounding North East (Gateshead, Sunderland, Durham, North/South Shields, Northumberland) as the core geography, with a radius setting around the showroom (NE28 6HA) as an alternative to naming towns individually. Areas further afield are priced individually, not free — worth a lower bid or separate campaign if Amfa wants to test further-out demand.

Audience note from the KB: Amfa isn't premium-only — the fitted-kitchen line explicitly targets budget-conscious and investment-property buyers too. Keep two registers available in ad copy (see §4) rather than luxury-only framing throughout.

## 2. Campaign structure

One Search campaign, ad groups by service line (the real eight categories — don't invent others):

| Ad group | Priority | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Bespoke Kitchens | Launch | Highest ticket (from £2,000), matches premium/non-standard-layout audience |
| Fitted Kitchens | Launch | Different buyer (budget-conscious, investment property, new-build) — separate ad group, separate messaging |
| Wardrobe & Bedroom Storage | Launch | Fitted / sliding door / walk-in / bedroom furniture |
| Bathroom Vanity Units | Launch | Lowest ticket (from £1,000) — good for volume/testing |
| Home Office Furniture | Phase 2 | Smaller, more seasonal demand |
| Hallway & Utility Room Furniture | Phase 2 | Niche search volume, likely low competition/CPC |
| Custom Storage Solutions | Phase 2 | Catch-all for anything not fitting the above |
| Commercial Furniture | Separate campaign | Different buyer (B2B — office/retail/hotel), different budget conversation and likely a different landing page; recommend as its own campaign later rather than folded into a consumer one |

Recommend launching with the 4 "Launch" ad groups first, adding the rest once there's real performance data to learn from.

## 6. Landing pages

Recommend sending each ad group to its matching category page on `amfa.uk` (e.g. Bespoke Kitchens ad group → `/kitchens`), not the homepage — better relevance for Quality Score and a shorter path to the "Get a free quote" form. Confirm each target page's copy issues (§0) are fixed before launch.

## 7. Budget guidance

**Illustrative only — no Keyword Planner or live Ads account data available this session. Get real CPC/volume estimates before committing a number.** As a general UK benchmark, "kitchen"/"fitted furniture" search terms in a regional market like this typically run somewhere in the low-to-mid single-digit £ CPC range, with more specific long-tail terms (e.g. "bespoke bathroom vanity unit Newcastle") usually cheaper than head terms. A starting monthly budget in the region of £600–£1,500 split across the 4 launch ad groups would be a reasonable test size to gather enough data to optimise from — but this is a planning estimate, not a quote, and should be validated against Keyword Planner and Amfa's actual marketing budget once account access exists.
