2. WHAT PERSONAL DATA WE COLLECT

We collect personal data you give us directly, for example when you:
- request a free quote or send us an enquiry through this website;
- contact us by phone, email, or in person at our showroom;
- book a measuring visit.

This may include your name, email address, phone number, and details about your project or space (for example, room dimensions, style preferences, or photos you choose to share with us).

We do not collect payment card details through this website.

3. COOKIES AND ANALYTICS

This website is built on the Tilda platform. We use Tilda's built-in website statistics to understand how the site is used, configured so that no tracking cookie is set for this purpose. We do not use Google Analytics, Facebook Pixel, or similar third-party advertising trackers on this site.

4. HOW WE USE YOUR DATA

We use your personal data to:
- respond to your enquiry or quote request;
- arrange and carry out measuring visits and consultations;
- provide you with a quote and, if you go ahead, deliver the furniture and installation service you order;
- keep you updated on the status of your order;
- improve our website and services.

Our legal basis for this is that it is necessary to take steps at your request before entering into a contract with you, and/or to perform a contract with you once you place an order. Where we use your data for anything else (for example, to tell you about other Amfa products or services), we will only do so with your consent, and you can withdraw that consent at any time.

5. SHARING YOUR DATA

We do not sell your personal data. Delivery and installation are carried out in-house by Amfa's own team, not third-party subcontractors. We may share your data with:
- our professional advisers (for example, accountants or IT and website service providers), where necessary for running our business;
- law enforcement or regulators, where we are required to by law.

6. HOW LONG WE KEEP YOUR DATA

We keep your personal data for as long as necessary to respond to your enquiry, fulfil any order, and meet our legal and accounting obligations. Where an enquiry or quote request does not lead to an order, we keep that data for up to 24 months before deleting it, unless you ask us to delete it sooner or we are required to keep it longer to meet a legal obligation.

7. DATA SECURITY

We take reasonable technical and organisational steps to protect your personal data against unauthorised access, loss, or misuse.

8. YOUR RIGHTS

Under UK data protection law, you have the right to:
- ask us what personal data we hold about you, and get a copy of it;
- ask us to correct inaccurate data;
- ask us to delete your data, in certain circumstances;
- object to, or ask us to restrict, certain uses of your data;
- ask us to transfer your data to another organisation, in certain circumstances;
- withdraw your consent at any time, where we rely on consent.

To exercise any of these rights, contact us using the details in Section 1.

If you are unhappy with how we have handled your personal data, you have the right to complain to the Information Commissioner's Office (ICO), the UK's data protection regulator, at ico.org.uk.

9. CHILDREN

Our website and services are aimed at adults. We do not knowingly collect personal data from children.

10. INTERNATIONAL TRANSFERS

Some of the suppliers we use to run this website and our business (for example, our website hosting platform) may store or process personal data outside the UK. Where this happens, we ensure appropriate safeguards are in place, such as contractual protections recognised under UK data protection law, so your data continues to receive an equivalent standard of protection.
