**Company:** Amfa Furniture Ltd · **Channel:** Website copy (`amfa.uk`, live) · **Status:** ready to apply · **Date:** 2026-10-07
**Source:** Minda confirmed delivery is within 50 miles, 2026-10-07. Matches the live measuring-visit wording and the Google Ads 50-mile radius around NE28 6HA (`CR-GA-0002`). Helen has no CMS access — this is for the developer to apply.

## Change 1 — Process page (`/process`), Delivery section

**Find (exact, live text):**
> We deliver across our full service area. If your property is outside our standard region, delivery and its cost are discussed individually.

**Replace with:**
> We deliver within 50 miles of our office. If your property is outside this area, delivery and its cost are discussed individually.

**Why:** the live text refers to a "service area" and "standard region" that no page defines. The replacement uses the same wording pattern as the measuring-visit sentence two sections above ("within 50 miles of our office … If your property is outside this area …"), so the two read as one policy.
