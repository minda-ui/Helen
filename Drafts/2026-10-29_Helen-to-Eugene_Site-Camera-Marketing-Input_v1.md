## 1. Content types worth having (most to least useful)
1. Time-lapse build video, one per flagship project (website + LinkedIn).
2. Before / during / after stills from the same fixed frame (case studies, portfolio pages).
3. 10–20 s social clips cut from the time-lapse (LinkedIn first).
4. Client progress reel (private link, not public marketing).

## 2. Capture spec that helps
- **Angle:** one wide fixed view per project showing the whole space, locked once placed (moving it mid-job breaks the time-lapse). A second view is a bonus.
- **Resolution:** 4K stills if the camera allows, 1080p minimum. Shoot landscape; crop to vertical for social later.
- **Interval:** one still every 5–10 min in working daylight hours is enough for a fit-out. Keep the original stills, not only the video.
- **Timestamp:** none burned in on the master (fine on copies). Keep the lens clean and power steady.
- **Retention:** keep stills for the whole job and until the case study is done. Marketing copies are separate files, so monitoring deletion does not remove them and only cleared footage is kept.
- **Keep out of frame where possible:** faces, vehicle plates, neighbouring properties, client signage or unreleased product, screens, paperwork.
