## 1. Claims & compliance (ASA/CAP) — clean

No unsubstantiated superlatives found anywhere across the nine pages ("best", "leading",
"award-winning" — none appear). Every claim reviewed is either a concrete, checkable fact (warranty
length, price floor, manufacturing time, materials) or a soft, non-comparative statement ("stylish,
practical solutions"). Pricing is correctly framed as "from £X" rather than a fixed price, which is
the right way to advertise a bespoke service — this is solid, compliant copy as drafted.

**To verify before launch (not a copy fix, a factual sign-off):** the following are specific,
checkable public commitments once live — worth Minda confirming each is still accurate at go-live,
since the PDFs may have been drafted ahead of final sign-off:

| Claim | Where it appears |
|---|---|
| Warranty "up to 10 years" on furniture and hardware | Kitchens, Home Office, Hallway & Utility, Bathroom Vanity pages |
| Prices "from £2,000" (kitchens), "from £1,500" (home office / hallway & utility), "from £1,000" (bathroom vanity) | Same four pages |
| Manufacturing time 2–8 weeks depending on product | Same four pages |
| Free measuring visit within 50 miles of the showroom | Design & Process page |

## 2. Real issues worth fixing

- **Typo, twice:** "Get a free **qoute** →" (should be "quote") — Kitchens page and Home Office page.
- **Typo, twice:** "Modern **syle**" (should be "style") — in the six-point differentiator list, on
  both the Home page and the About page.
- **Capitalisation inconsistency:** body copy on the Home page reads "Living room furniture → Media
  walls & **Tv** units→", but the footer navigation correctly reads "Media Walls & **TV** Units." Pick
  one and use it everywhere.
- **Awkward phrasing on the About page:** *"AMFA – is a furniture company with our own production
  facility..."* mixes third person ("AMFA... is a furniture company") with first person ("our own
  production facility") in the same sentence. Reads like a rough draft/translation pass. Worth
  smoothing since About is a high-visibility page — e.g. *"AMFA is a furniture company with its own
  production facility..."* or recast fully in first person ("We're a furniture company...").

## 3. PDF-extraction artifacts — probably not real bugs, but worth a human eyeball

The Home page's reviews section extracted with duplicated/garbled text: the same "Elizabeth S. —
Bespoke sloped shelving unit" review appears twice, with one copy cut off mid-sentence
("Everything was handled profession..."). Several footer navigation items also appear doubled
("Hallway Hallway & Utility & Utility Room Room Furniture", "Utility Utility room furniture room
furniture"). This is almost certainly just how a PDF export flattens a carousel or multi-column layout
— not necessarily an actual site bug.

That said, it raises a real question worth asking directly: **is there currently only one client
testimonial** (Elizabeth S.)? If a reviews section shows a single review repeated to look like a
carousel, that will read as padding once real visitors compare it against a genuinely populated
section. Worth confirming whether more testimonials are coming before launch — and per the charter,
Helen would need evidence of consent before drafting or citing any client testimonial by name.

## 4. Structural observation (not a defect)

Every service page repeats the same six differentiators (In-House manufacturing, Made for You, Built
to last, Residential & commercial, Modern style, Always on time), the same 4-step process, and
near-identical "materials, finishes and hardware" boilerplate. That's good for site-wide consistency
but makes the pages fairly formulaic if read back-to-back — normal and reasonable for a site like
this, just flagging in case more per-category personality is wanted later.
