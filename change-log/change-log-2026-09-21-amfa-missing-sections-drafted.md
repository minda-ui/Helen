page, gave two options — a version with a stated starting price (if Minda wants one) and a version that
states pricing is individual per project (the more common approach for B2B furniture work) — for her to
pick between.

opening date) was drafted as a placeholder rather than answered, since committing publicly to hitting a
commercial deadline carries more reputational weight than a missed residential delivery date, and isn't
